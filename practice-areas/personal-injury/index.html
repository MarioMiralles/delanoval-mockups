Personal injury

An accident can change your year in a moment. We handle the claim so you can focus on getting well.

Questions

Common questions

General information only, not legal advice. Every case is different.

How long do I have to file an injury claim in Florida?

For most negligence claims arising after March 24, 2023, Florida law allows two years from the date of the injury to file a lawsuit. Some claims have shorter deadlines, so speak with an attorney early.

What should I do after a car accident?

Get medical care, call the police, photograph the scene and the vehicles, and collect contact details from witnesses. In Florida, seeing a doctor within 14 days of the crash matters for your PIP benefits.

What if I was partly at fault?

You may still recover, reduced by your share of fault. Under Florida law since 2023, a person found more than 50 percent at fault generally cannot recover damages in most negligence cases.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurance company?

Be careful. Adjusters may ask for a recorded statement or offer a quick settlement before you know the full extent of your injuries. It is usually best to speak with an attorney first.

Will my case go to court?

Most injury claims resolve through negotiation, but we prepare every case as if it might go to trial.
