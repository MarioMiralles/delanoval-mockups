FAQ

Questions we hear every week

How much does it cost to talk to a lawyer?

Your first consultation is free and there is no obligation. [Confirm fee structure, for example contingency fees for injury and property claims.]

My insurance company denied my claim. Is it too late?

Not necessarily. Florida law sets deadlines for property claims and lawsuits, and they depend on your policy and the date of the loss. Call us as soon as you can so we can review your timeline.

What should I do right after a car accident?

Get medical care, report the accident, take photos and collect witness information. In Florida, seeing a doctor within 14 days matters for your PIP benefits. Avoid giving a recorded statement to the other driver's insurer before you speak with a lawyer.

Will my case go to court?

Many cases are resolved through negotiation without a trial. If yours needs to go to court, we will prepare you and be with you at every step.
