Our firm

We meet with our clients and work alongside them at every step, so the outcome is one they helped shape.

De La Noval, P.A. was founded on the principles of proactive and aggressive advocacy. Each case receives the dedicated attention it deserves, and those values have guided the firm since it opened its doors.

Our partners began their careers on the other side: Lazaro defending insurance companies, Elizabeth prosecuting cases as a Miami-Dade Assistant State Attorney. Today they use what they learned there to build stronger cases for their clients.

We work in English and Spanish from our office in Doral, and represent clients throughout Florida in state and federal court.

How we work

What every client can expect

Four commitments that shape every case, whether it is a denied roof claim or a custody agreement.

01

Plain language

We explain your options in words you can act on, in English or Spanish, so every decision is yours to make.

02

Your attorney, directly

You speak with the attorney handling your case. Questions get answered by the person who knows your file.

03

Updates at every step

You will always know where your case stands and what happens next, without having to chase anyone for it.

04

Ready for court

We prepare each case from the first day as if it will be argued before a judge. That preparation changes how the other side negotiates.
